# ATSRS legal-footer adaptation — 10 August 2026

## Reference reviewed

- Remindax GDPR Compliance
- Remindax Terms of Service
- Remindax Privacy Statement
- Remindax Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

## Take now

- A clearly separated footer legal row that remains visible without registration.
- Dedicated, readable pages for data protection, service terms, privacy rights and security reporting.
- A concise summary at the start of each page, followed by anchored sections.
- Security-report instructions that ask for reproducible evidence while protecting production users and data.
- Cross-links between the legal pages so a visitor does not reach a dead end.

## Adapt for ATSRS

- Explain separate Personal, Corporate and Candidate responsibilities.
- Treat profile visibility, preview, download, share links and company access as separate permissions.
- State that AI output requires human review.
- State that expiry status is not document authentication, legal compliance, medical fitness or role eligibility.
- Explain Corporate customer responsibility for lawful personnel and candidate processing.
- Keep plan and credit language flexible until paid billing terms are final.

## Do not copy or claim

- AWS region or country-local storage claims without verified infrastructure evidence.
- External GDPR certification or a blanket “fully compliant” statement.
- A formal DPA, staff GDPR training programme or sub-processor commitments that are not yet documented.
- Guaranteed security response times, bounty rewards, recognition or legal safe harbour.
- Google Calendar/API disclosures because ATSRS does not currently offer that integration.
- Generic website-linking and iframe clauses that do not address ATSRS platform risks.

## Implemented information architecture

1. Data Protection & GDPR — transparent overview without a certification claim.
2. Terms of Use — ATSRS-specific account, content, sharing, AI, plan and acceptable-use rules.
3. Privacy Notice — existing detailed processing notice.
4. Data Rights — existing access, correction, export and deletion process.
5. Report a Security Issue — scoped, production-safe reporting policy.
