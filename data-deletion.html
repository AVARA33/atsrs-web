Data rights and deletion

Last reviewed: 4 August 2026

You may ask ATSRS to provide access to, correct, export, restrict or delete personal data, subject to identity verification and applicable legal exceptions.

How to submit a request

  1. Email anaragasiyev@gmail.com using the subject “ATSRS Privacy Request”.
  2. State the account email, whether the request concerns a Personal or Corporate workspace, and the right you want to exercise.
  3. Do not email a password, one-time code, full identity-document scan, PIN or authentication token.

Verification and response

ATSRS may ask you to verify control of the relevant account or provide limited additional information. ATSRS will respond within the period required by applicable law. Complex or multiple requests may take longer where the law permits, and ATSRS will explain any permitted extension.

Corporate workspace information

If a company placed your candidate or personnel information in ATSRS, that company is normally the controller. ATSRS may forward the request to the company or assist it in responding. You may also contact the company directly.

What deletion means

After a verified deletion or account-closure request, ATSRS targets account data and uploaded content for removal from active systems within 30 days unless retention is necessary for security, another person’s rights, a legal obligation or a legal claim. Residual copies may remain temporarily in access-restricted backups until the provider backup cycle overwrites them.

Before deletion: download any information you need and revoke active sharing links. Deletion may be irreversible and may remove documents, expiry history, CV content, profile settings and Corporate workspace records within the request’s lawful scope.
