FAQ

Clear answers about accounts, document handling, profile sharing and ATSRS plans.

Can I inspect ATSRS without registering?

Yes. Public pages explain the available Personal, Candidate and Corporate workflows. Registration is required only when you create a workspace.

Does AI save document details automatically?

No. AI scan suggests fields for review. You confirm or correct every value before saving.

Who can see my Personal profile?

You choose profile visibility. Candidate directory visibility is available from the Bronze plan and does not expose private documents automatically.

Can a company download every document?

No. Preview, download permission, share links and company access are controlled separately.

Do Plans, FAQ or Contact sign me out?

No. When opened from your account sidebar, these pages remain inside your authenticated ATSRS workspace.

Can I purchase additional capacity?

Additional capacity can be arranged separately for eligible products without changing the whole plan. Published pricing remains unchanged until add-on prices are officially released.
