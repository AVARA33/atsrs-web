QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Know what you are choosing.

Can I inspect ATSRS without registering?

Yes. This page explains the available Personal, Candidate and Corporate workflows. Registration is required only when you create a workspace.

Does AI save document details automatically?

No. AI scan suggests fields for review. You confirm or correct every value before saving.

Who can see my Personal profile?

You choose profile visibility. Candidate directory visibility is available from the Bronze plan and does not expose private documents automatically.

Can a company download every document?

No. Preview, download permission, share links and company access are controlled separately.
