Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy will apply when ATSRS paid billing opens. No paid checkout is currently active.

Prepared: 15 August 2026Applies: when paid checkout opens
CONTENTS

1. Cancelling renewal

Cancellation stops future renewal. Unless the checkout states otherwise, access remains available until the end of the already-paid billing period. Deleting an account is not a substitute for cancelling a recurring subscription.

2. Refund requests

Requests should include the ATSRS account email, transaction reference, charge date, amount and reason. Do not send card numbers, CVV values, passwords or one-time codes. ATSRS will review whether the charge was duplicated, unauthorised, technically unsuccessful or otherwise refundable under applicable law and the checkout terms.

3. Duplicate or incorrect charges

A confirmed duplicate charge or an amount different from the amount authorised at checkout will be corrected or refunded. A pending bank authorisation is not always a completed charge and may first need to expire or be released by the bank.

4. Consumed services and add-on credits

Where legally permitted, amounts for already-consumed AI scans, message credits or other immediately delivered digital services may be non-refundable if the user expressly requested immediate performance. This does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds will be sent through the original payment channel. ATSRS will record the refund promptly, but the customer’s bank controls the time needed for the amount to appear.

6. Statutory rights and disputes

Nothing in this policy removes rights that cannot legally be excluded. Users should contact ATSRS first so the transaction can be investigated. Chargeback or bank-dispute rights remain subject to the cardholder’s bank and applicable rules.

Contact details: the final billing support channel and company registration details will be published before paid checkout opens.
